# TeslaMate Legal & Governance

This repository contains the official legal documents, licensing terms, and trademark policies for the TeslaMate project.

## ⚖️ Contributor License Agreement (CLA)
To ensure the long-term sustainability of TeslaMate and to protect the project from legal challenges, we require all contributors to sign our **Fiduciary License Agreement (FLA 2.0)**.

* **[Link to Individual CLA](./CLA.md)**
* **Why do we need this?** It guarantees that TeslaMate will **always remain Free Software** (AGPL-3.0) and allows the [teslamate-org](https://github.com/teslamate-org) to legally defend the project against license violations.

## 🏷️ Trademark Policy
TeslaMate is a community-driven project. To prevent user confusion and protect our brand, we have established a clear Trademark Policy.

* **[Read the Trademark Policy](https://github.com/teslamate-org/teslamate/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md)**
