# TeslaMate Legal & Governance

This repository contains the contributor agreements of the TeslaMate project. The license and the trademark policy are part of the [TeslaMate repository](https://github.com/teslamate-org/teslamate).

## ⚖️ Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

To ensure the long-term sustainability of TeslaMate and to protect the project from legal challenges, we require all contributors to sign our **Fiduciary License Agreement (FLA 2.0)**. The CLA Assistant bot asks you to sign it on your first pull request.

* **[Link to Individual CLA](./CLA.md)**
* **[Link to Entity CLA](./entity-CLA.md)**
* **Why do we need this?** It guarantees that TeslaMate will **always remain Free Software** and allows the [teslamate-org](https://github.com/teslamate-org) to legally defend the project against license violations.

## 🏷️ Trademark Policy

TeslaMate is a community-driven project. To prevent user confusion and protect our brand, we have established a clear Trademark Policy.

* **[Read the Trademark Policy](https://github.com/teslamate-org/teslamate/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md)**
